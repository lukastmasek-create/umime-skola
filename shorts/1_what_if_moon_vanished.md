# What If the Moon Vanished?
Facts: Moon stabilizes Earth's tilt (~23.4°) over millions of years; tides ~2/3 lunar, 1/3 solar; days were ~6 h at Earth's birth, Moon slows rotation; without Moon tides shrink, nights darker, tilt may wobble over long time; no instant doom.

What if the Moon just vanished tonight?

First, you'd notice. The night sky goes dark. Really dark. Wolves, owls, and sea turtles all lose their night light.

Next, the oceans. Two thirds of our tides come from the Moon. Without it, tides shrink. Only the Sun's pull is left.

Coasts would change. Tidepools dry. Fish that wait for the big spring tide miss their cue.

Now the slow part. The Moon is a steadying hand. It keeps Earth tilted at a calm twenty-three degrees.

Without it, the tilt could start to wobble. Over millions of years, seasons might swing wildly. Ice at the equator. Desert at the poles.

Days would change too. The Moon slowly drags Earth's spin. Without it, that slows down.

So no, you wouldn't die tomorrow. But slowly, Earth would get stranger.

Keep an eye on the Moon. It's doing more than you think.
