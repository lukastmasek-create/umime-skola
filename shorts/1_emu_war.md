# The Great Emu War (Australia, 1932)
Facts: Western Australia, Nov 1932; ~20,000 emus wrecked wheat farms; veterans sent with 2 Lewis guns, ~10k rounds, Maj. Meredith; emus split into small groups, fast, took many bullets; withdrawn, retried, bounty system worked better; farmers later got fences + bounties.

Australia once declared war on birds. And lost.

It's 1932. Farmers in Western Australia are broke. Then twenty thousand emus walk in and eat the wheat.

So the government sends soldiers. Two machine guns. Ten thousand bullets.

The plan is simple. Emus are big, loud, and stand in a crowd. Easy targets.

Turns out, emus are not dumb. They split into tiny groups. They sprint at thirty miles an hour. A bullet barely slows them down.

The soldiers chase. The emus scatter. The trucks bounce. The guns jam.

After a week, the army gives up. Birds nearly untouched.

Parliament was embarrassed. They sent the troops back anyway. Still no luck.

In the end, what worked? Not war. Bounties for hunters, and better fences for farms.

Emus: one. Army: zero.
