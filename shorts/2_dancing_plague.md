# The Dancing Plague (Strasbourg, 1518)
Facts: July 1518; Frau Troffea began dancing in street; within a month ~400 joined; city built stage, hired musicians and guards (bad idea); some collapsed/died of exhaustion; leading theory: mass psychogenic illness under famine stress; shrine to St. Vitus ended it.

One summer afternoon in 1518, a woman stepped into a Strasbourg street and started to dance. She did not stop.

Not for an hour. Not for a day. Not for a week.

Soon, dozens joined her. Then hundreds. Nobody could say why.

The city had a plan. Maybe they needed more dancing? So officials built a stage. Hired musicians. Cleared the square.

It did not help. People kept dancing until their feet bled. Some collapsed. Some never got back up.

So the plan changed. No more music. Pray instead. Priests marched the dancers to a shrine and handed them red shoes.

And it worked. The dancing slowly stopped.

Why did it happen? Nobody knows for sure. Starvation, fear, and stress may have broken a whole town's mind at once.

Strange, sad, and completely real.
