# What If All the Oceans Drained?
Facts: oceans cover ~71% of Earth; avg depth ~3.7 km; Mariana Trench ~11 km; oceans absorb ~90% of excess heat and ~25% of CO2; produce ~half of oxygen (phytoplankton); lots of Earth's life lives there; ocean floor has mountains, ridges, volcanoes.

What if every ocean drained away tomorrow?

First, the view. Seventy percent of the planet turns into a vast, dry basin. Mountains taller than Everest, rising from the seabed. A trench seven miles deep.

Then, the quiet. Almost everything that lives in the sea dies. Fish. Whales. Coral. Billions of animals, gone.

Next, the air. Tiny sea plants make about half the oxygen you breathe. No plankton, less oxygen.

Then, the heat. Oceans soak up most of our extra heat and a big share of our carbon. Without them, the planet swings hot and cold. Fast.

And the rain. Most clouds start over the sea. No water, no clouds. Deserts everywhere.

Within a few years, the land would struggle too. Farms fail. Cities thirst.

The ocean isn't just scenery. It's the engine.

Next time you see the sea, say thanks.
